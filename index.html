I'm Siddhant Yadav, a software engineer from Kathmandu, Nepal. Online I go by SiddTheCoder, or just Sidd.

I'm a founder, a member of the board of directors and the CTO of Softmato Technology Pvt Ltd. I work across the whole stack: React, Next.js, Node.js and MongoDB on the web, React Native on mobile, Electron on desktop, and Python with FastAPI for AI, from LLM integrations to voice assistants.
